If someone doesn’t have the V

No green V does not automatically mean “bot.” It means they haven’t shown a Vision Verified proof. Common reasons:

  • Under 18 — we block minors from verifying (by design)
  • Couldn’t verify — ID/face checks failed or weren’t available
  • Wouldn’t verify — they chose not to
  • Bot or fake — or an account that never completed a real-person check

That clarity helps people know when they may be talking to a minor (who can’t get a V) or an account that never proved it’s human — so communities can spot and flag likely bots more easily.

Where DL back-barcode scans are restricted

Some U.S. jurisdictions limit commercial scanning of the barcode / magnetic strip on a driver’s license. Those users are not locked out. They are simply exempt from scanning the back of the license. No second ID. They keep using their one approved photo ID (front of the DL or state ID is enough in those places), with live face match. Images are deleted when verification finishes.

The exempt-state list is maintained with counsel (laws change). Product rule: the system reads the ID and prompts for the back only when the state allows it; otherwise skip the back scan — never force an illegal scan, and never require a passport or any second ID to compensate.

Not legal advice. This is a public checklist of who to talk to before real ID or face scanning goes live. Galaxy entry can stay design-only until that path is clear.

  1. Privacy / biometrics lawyer (best first call) — someone who knows face-scan and ID rules (often called biometric privacy / “BIPA”-style laws).
    Hurdle: rules differ by state; consent, notice, and delete rules are strict; mistakes can be expensive.
  2. Consumer / identity lawyer — firms that advise companies doing ID checks (banks, marketplaces).
    Hurdle: they may push heavy “know your customer” rules that are bigger than a free X trust badge — more scope and cost.
  3. Driver’s license / DMV data rules — counsel who knows when barcode / strip scans are restricted.

  4. Vendor legal pack — Persona, Onfido, or similar: ask for compliance docs and which places they support.
    Hurdle: their paperwork helps, but it does not replace your own counsel for how you use the tool.

    Hurdle: entering the contest and turning on real scanning are different legal moments.
  6. Short specialist consult — state bar referral, tech-law clinic, or a 1–2 hour paid consult with someone who has done face/ID work.
    Hurdle: cheap general advice may miss biometric rules — ask for that experience up front.

How we make it hard for bots and AI to fool verification

This is the product stack Vision Verified must deliver (design-only until counsel/vendor clearance for live capture):

  1. Live camera — live session only, not an uploaded photo or old video file. In the live verify view: show your face first, then hold your license in front of your face so both are in frame.
  2. Real ID — one approved gov photo ID (US DL, state ID, or passport). The system reads the license/ID and prompts to show the back only if their state allows barcode/back scans; otherwise it skips the back scan (never a second ID).
  3. Face matches the ID photo
  4. PAD (anti-spoof) — catches masks, printouts, screens, and many deepfake presentation tricks. The heavy hitter vs AI fakes.
  5. Random gestures — scrunch nose, touch nose, and similar, with “choose a different exercise.” Helps stop lazy bots; not enough alone.
  6. Injection defense — block fake video piped in instead of a real webcam.
  7. Vaulted unique face code + signed ticket — QR is a signed ticket id only; zero duplicate matches after mint; same face elsewhere gets flagged.
  8. Fail closed — any doubt = no pass. No soft VISION pass.
  9. Delete photos when done — no ID file and no personal info from the ID kept. We keep the unique geometric face proof (signed QR / ticket), not a photo or license copy.

Negatives (what we will not do): CAPTCHA / slider / puzzle as security; gestures alone as the whole bar; second-ID requirements; soft passes; putting the face map inside the QR; storing a recoverable face photo as the “secret”; keeping DL details after verify.
